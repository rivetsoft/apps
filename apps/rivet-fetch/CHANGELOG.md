## Unreleased

- **rivetfetch, a new program**: this computer at a glance in the terminal, beside Rivet the
  mascot, in the desktop's accent. It shows the essentials by default; screens, theme, disk,
  battery and the Rivet apps installed are there to add.
- Choose what to show and in what order with `--modules`, and the picture with `--logo`
  (`mascot`, `none` or a fastfetch logo file), on the command line or in
  `~/.config/rivet-fetch/settings`.
