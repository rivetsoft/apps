# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Rivet Cinema, a new app**: plays video and music files, folders and playlists, with a queue
  you can reorder, repeat and shuffle.
- **The seek bar shows a frame** from wherever the pointer is, with the chapter's name.
- **Tracks and subtitles**: choose the audio language and the subtitles from menus, add a
  subtitle file, and move subtitles earlier or later. A subtitle file named like the video is
  loaded by itself.
- **Starts where you left off** in anything over a minute long. Press Home to start over.
- **Music** shows its cover, its title, artist and album, and the queue, in a smaller window.
- **Fullscreen** hides the controls and the pointer while it plays. **Keep on Top** puts the
  video in a small window of its own.
- **Media keys and the panel's player controls** work, and the screen stays awake while a
  video plays.
- Keys like other players: Space to pause, the arrow keys to seek and change the volume, F for
  fullscreen, [ and ] for the speed.
