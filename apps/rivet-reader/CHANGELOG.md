# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Screen readers and the keyboard**: Orca and other screen readers can read the window's
  buttons, menus, check boxes and text fields, and Tab shows a ring around the control that has
  the keyboard. Shift+F10 opens the right-click menu of the control that has the keyboard.
  With higher contrast or less motion asked for in the desktop's settings, edges and secondary
  text get stronger and animations stop.
- **Rivet Reader, a new app**: read PDFs and comic books, in one long scroll of pages or one page
  at a time, with page thumbnails and the document's outline in the sidebar.
- **Find** lights up every match in the document, and Enter goes from one to the next.
- **Select and copy** text from a PDF, and follow its links, inside the document or out to the
  web.
- **Dark pages** show a PDF light on dark in your desktop's colors, keeping colored text in its
  color and pictures as they are.
- **Zoom** to the width, the whole page or any size, rotate pages, and print a PDF through the
  desktop's print dialog.
- Each file opens again on the page you left it at.
